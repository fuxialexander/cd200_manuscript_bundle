Relative ATAC signal at the CD200 promoter and TEAD motif-containing enhancer. Panels summarize 1-kb windows centered on the annotated transcript start and TEAD motif midpoint, respectively. Orange dots represent all 120 tumor donors from the seven TCGA cancer types displayed in Fig. 4D; violins summarize their distributions and black bars mark medians. Blue lines labeled Normal show the corresponding cell-type pseudobulk reference. Normal identities and source/match classes are provided in cohort_summary.tsv; these include fetal metanephric cells for KIRP/KIRC, club cells for LUSC, and uterine smooth muscle for UCEC. These are contextual references rather than patient-matched controls. Values retain the original figure normalization: track-specific 1-Mb background subtraction, clipping at zero, and division by the track-specific background-subtracted maximum. The plotted differences describe relative signal, not absolute accessibility or fold changes. Mean-signal and maximum-bin-signal versions use the same windows; no tumor donor is excluded based on signal or noise.
